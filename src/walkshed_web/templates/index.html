    About this map

    Each shaded area is the street network you can walk from a rapid transit station in 15 minutes at 4.5 km/h. Entrances of one station are merged. Metro, Marmaray, tram, funicular, cable car and Metrobüs are included.

    Click a line to toggle it. Ctrl-click or Cmd-click isolates it. Double-click zooms to it. Press / to search, Esc to close.
